The Limitations of Generative AI

What these systems cannot (yet) do, and why

Nadia Yvette · survey, ~14 pages · CC BY-SA 4.0

A catalogue of what generative AI systems systematically cannot do, organized by mechanism rather than as a laundry list: statistical failures (confabulation, context, calibration), dynamical failures (learning while acting), and normative failures (rules the system cannot even represent). Written for readers without a machine-learning background, with one discipline held throughout — every claim separated into what is observed, what is explained, and what is merely narrated. The catalogue extends past the models to their delivery mechanism (hosted services, withheld weights, and the digital-sovereignty consequences) and to the incentive structure behind vendor performance claims.
