# Licensing policy for gen-ai-limits

Same category-based scheme as the MOWGLI project:

| Category | License |
|---|---|
| Source code (Makefile, scripts) | EUPL-1.2 — https://eupl.eu/1.2/en/ |
| Document text (`.tex`), bibliography (`.bib`) | CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ |

Canonical license texts are not redistributed in this repository; the links
above point to the authoritative texts. SPDX identifiers: `EUPL-1.2`,
`CC-BY-SA-4.0`.

Cited papers remain under their own copyrights; quotations in the write-up are
used with attribution under fair-use/citation norms and do not relicence the
cited material.
